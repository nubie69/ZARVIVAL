A few tricks to stay alive

Find your footing. Tap jump for a short hop, or hold it for more height. Late jumps and early landing presses get a little forgiveness.

Read the fight. Amber warnings mean an attack is coming. Interrupt with a hit, or dodge with Shift. Dash costs 20 power; power attacks cost 30.

Keep the pressure on. Hold Attack to keep swinging. Chain hits for combos, and slash incoming cannonballs to block them.

Explore the forest. Red potions and crates restore health. Blue potions restore power. The radar tracks enemies, and cleared levels save automatically.
